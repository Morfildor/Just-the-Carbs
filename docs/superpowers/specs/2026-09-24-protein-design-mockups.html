Protein as a second reading, never a second answer

Mockups for the optional Protein layer in Just the Carbs. Every surface, size and colour below is the app's own token (Theme.kt), so what you see is what the implementation renders. Tap any Show protein chip on the Home mocks to flip it.

Companion to docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-24-protein-design.md. Photos are stand-ins (initials plates); the real screens load Open Food Facts photography into the same box.

1 · Home: the Protein chip closes the entry cluster

The two scan tiles and Enter manually are the three ways in. The chip sits on that last row, trailing edge, as the one modifier of what those ways in will read. Off, it is a quiet pill in ink with a hairline edge and an outlined egg. On, it takes the paired container the app already uses for a selected state, the egg fills, and the primary tile's subtitle says what the next scan will read.

The chip, close up

Anatomy48dp pill (the app's own chip height, so no extra touch padding), 12dp leading inset, 20dp egg, 8dp gap, labelLarge SemiBold label Show protein, 16dp trailing inset. Role: switch; the platform speaks On / Off.
OffFill surfaceContainerLow, label in onSurface, outlined egg in onSurfaceVariant, and a 1dp hairline: outlineVariant in Light (the recent cards' own edge), outline in Dark (the page-luminance rule of JtcValueButton). A control that stands alone needs an edge; a 1.05:1 fill cannot bound it.
OnFill secondaryContainer, label and filled egg in onSecondaryContainer. No border in Light; in Dark the outline hairline stays (3.5:1 against the fill), because the rule keys on the page, not on the state. No check mark, no second colour.
MotionContainer, content and glyph cross in one beat, 220ms ease-out-quart. No scale, no bounce, no haptic: the app's haptics belong to scan outcomes.

2 · The mark: an egg, outlined off and filled on

The app's icons are Material Filled glyphs; its one existing on/off icon is the favourite star, outlined until set. Protein follows exactly that grammar with Icons.Outlined.Egg and Icons.Filled.Egg, which ship in the icon library the app already bundles (resolved through the Compose BOM to 1.7.8, verified in the Gradle cache). The chip draws it at 20dp. If a device screenshot read at arm's length shows a water drop rather than an egg, the fallback is Icons.Outlined.EggAlt / Icons.Filled.EggAlt from the same artifact (the yolk makes it unambiguous), shown as alt below. An egg is the one protein symbol that is neither a meat cut nor a gym weight, and it stays legible at 18dp.

6 · Why it reads as one answer and one reading

Carbs figure
72sp Bold, coral, stacked under its eyebrow. The only coloured number.
Protein figure
18sp SemiBold, ink, beside its eyebrow, after the carb block. One quarter the size, no colour, one line.
No online value
15sp Regular, supporting ink. Names the source, so it is a fact about the record and not about the package, and it never reads as a figure.
Chip on
The paired container every selected control already uses. A state, not a brand colour.

Four separations at once: scale (72 against 18), weight (Bold against SemiBold), hue (coral against ink) and layout (stacked against inline). Any one of them survives on its own, which is what keeps the hierarchy legible in monochrome, at 1.8x, and in Dark.
